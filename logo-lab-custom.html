Letterform Construction Specs

Technique: Stroked SVG paths — stroke-width="14", stroke-linecap="square", stroke-linejoin="miter".
Grid: Each letter cell 80w × 152h viewBox units (cap-height ~120). Stroke weight 14 = 11.7% of cell width. Proportional spacing: A=75, L=80, I=60, G=80, N=80; total wordmark width ~375 units.
A: Flat-top apex via horizontal top bar + two slanted stems + mid-height crossbar. Apex is a T-junction.
L: Vertical stem + horizontal baseline bar. Square corner at junction.
I: Vertical stem with top and bottom serif bars. Centered in 60-unit cell at x=185.
G: Squared-C form. Left full-height vertical + top horizontal + right partial vertical + bottom horizontal + horizontal crossbar at 60% height. Opening on right between top bar and crossbar.
N: Two vertical stems connected by diagonal. All square terminals.
Colors: Navy #1B2A3D (light mode), White #ffffff (dark mode). C3 knot orange centerline #E8641B.
C3 Icon: Exact paths from assets/c3-icon.svg — unmodified.
